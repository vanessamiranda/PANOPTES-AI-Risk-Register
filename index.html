Framework coverage

Real data. Each risk category maps to a MITRE ATLAS technique, an OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications (2025) entry and NIST AI Risk Management Framework subcategories, as encoded in the HERMES attack library. Test results are from the executed run; the findings column counts synthetic register items in the same category.

Coverage of the MAP function is an acknowledged gap in the current test suite: HERMES exercises GOVERN, MEASURE and MANAGE subcategories, while system-context mapping activities sit upstream of adversarial testing. Stating what the evidence does not cover is part of the assurance method.
